# Generator Manual (synthetic sample, 20 kVA diesel set)

## Safety warnings
- Stop the engine and wait 10 minutes before opening any guard. Belts and fan stay hot.
- Disconnect the battery negative terminal before touching the belt or alternator.
- Never refuel while running. Keep fuel drums 5 m from the unit.

## Inspection steps (daily)
1. Check oil level on the dipstick; top up if below the lower mark.
2. Check coolant level with the engine cold.
3. Look at the drive belt for cracks, glazing or fraying.
4. Check fuel level and drain water from the fuel filter bowl.

## Troubleshooting
- Won't start: check battery voltage (should be above 12 V), fuel valve, and air filter.
- Starts then dies: water in fuel or blocked filter. Drain and replace the filter.
- Squealing or no charge: loose or worn drive belt. Inspect, then replace the belt.
- Overheating: low coolant or slipping fan belt. Stop the engine at once.

## Replacing the drive belt
1. Stop the engine, disconnect the battery negative, remove the belt guard (4 bolts).
2. Loosen the alternator pivot bolt and the adjuster bolt.
3. Push the alternator toward the engine and slip the old belt off the pulleys.
4. Fit the new belt (part GB-38) over the crank, fan and alternator pulleys.
5. Pull the alternator outward until belt deflection is about 10 mm at mid-span, then tighten the adjuster and pivot bolts.
6. Refit the guard, reconnect the battery, run 30 minutes and recheck tension.
Spare belts are kept in the store box.
